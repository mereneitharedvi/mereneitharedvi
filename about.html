The Storyteller

Mereneith Aredvi writes adult fiction about people who are difficult to save, difficult to love, and sometimes both.

Her stories move through mafia, dark romance, psychological thriller and crime, but resist the easy answers those genres can offer. The darkness is not the decoration. It is where the characters are tested.

The books are written for adults and may contain explicit sexual content, violence, criminal activity and other mature themes. Individual books will carry their own content advisories.
